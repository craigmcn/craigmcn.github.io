This week's instalment of Music Monday is [*Tequila Sunrise*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tequila_Sunrise_(Eagles_song)) by [Eagles](https://www.eagles.com/) from their 1973 album, [Desperado](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Desperado_(Eagles_album)). The [Alan Jackson](https://www.alanjackson.com/) version is from his 1993 album, [Common Thread: The Songs of the Eagles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Common_Thread:_The_Songs_of_the_Eagles).

Don Henley and Glenn Frey wrote the song in the first week of their partnership, naming it after a cocktail then popular in California bars. It was the first single from Desperado. Alan Jackson recorded his take for Common Thread, a Walden Woods Project fundraiser that went triple platinum.
