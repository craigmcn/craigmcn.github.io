This week's instalment of Music Monday is [*Valerie*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valerie_(Zutons_song)) by [The Zutons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Zutons) from their 2006 album, [Tired of Hanging Around](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tired_of_Hanging_Around). The [Amy Winehouse & Mark Ronson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Ronson) version is from their 2007 album, [Version](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Version_(album)).

Amy Winehouse became a fan of the Zutons single and was invited to join Mark Ronson's album of soul covers of guitar records. Ronson struggled to hear her singing it, yet the horn-driven Motown-style rework became a bigger UK hit than the original and is now the version most people know.
