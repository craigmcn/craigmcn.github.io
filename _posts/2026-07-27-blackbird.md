This week&#039;s instalment of Music Monday is [*Blackbird*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blackbird_(Beatles_song)) by [The Beatles](https://www.thebeatles.com) from their 1968 album, [The Beatles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Beatles_(album)). The [Sarah McLachlan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarah_McLachlan) version is from her 2002 album, [I Am Sam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I_Am_Sam_(soundtrack)).

Paul McCartney wrote and recorded this solo acoustic piece after Martin Luther King Jr.&#039;s assassination, using the blackbird as a symbol of the civil rights struggle in the American south. McLachlan&#039;s stripped down reading appeared on the I Am Sam soundtrack, an album of Beatles covers by contemporary artists.
