This week's instalment of Music Monday is [*Piece of My Heart*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piece_of_My_Heart) by [Erma Franklin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erma_Franklin) from her 1967 single, [Piece of My Heart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piece_of_My_Heart). The [Janis Joplin with Big Brother and the Holding Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Big_Brother_and_the_Holding_Company) version is from their 1968 album, [Cheap Thrills](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cheap_Thrills_(Big_Brother_and_the_Holding_Company_album)).

Erma Franklin, Aretha's older sister, cut the original in 1967 for producer Bert Berns, who co-wrote it with Jerry Ragovoy. Berns died that December and never heard Janis Joplin turn it into a raw, guitar-driven scream. Franklin said she didn't recognize the song when it came on the radio. Her version got a second life in 1992 when a Levi's ad sent it to number nine in the UK.
