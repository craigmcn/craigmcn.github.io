This week's instalment of Music Monday is [*Girls Just Want to Have Fun*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Girls_Just_Want_to_Have_Fun) by [Robert Hazard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Hazard) from his 1979 demo. The [Cyndi Lauper](https://www.cyndilauper.com/) version is from her 1983 album, [She's So Unusual](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/She%27s_So_Unusual).

Hazard wrote the song from a male point of view and recorded only a demo. Producer Rick Chertoff found it while looking for material for Lauper's solo debut. She reworked the lyrics, flipped the perspective and turned a leering novelty into an anthem. Her version reached number 2 in the US and number 1 in Canada.
