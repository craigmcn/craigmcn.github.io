This week's instalment of Music Monday is [*(There's) Always Something There to Remind Me*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/(There's)_Always_Something_There_to_Remind_Me) by [Lou Johnson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lou_Johnson_(singer)) from 1964, a non-album single. The [Naked Eyes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naked_Eyes) version is from their 1983 album, [Naked Eyes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burning_Bridges_(Naked_Eyes_album)).

Burt Bacharach and Hal David wrote this for Dionne Warwick as a demo but Lou Johnson took it to the charts first in 1964. Sandie Shaw's UK cover hit number one that same year but it took Naked Eyes' synth-pop version, cut in one take at Abbey Road, to finally crack the US top ten almost two decades later.
