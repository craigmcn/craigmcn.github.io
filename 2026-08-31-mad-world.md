This week's instalment of Music Monday is [*Mad World*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mad_World) by [Tears for Fears](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tears_for_Fears) from their 1983 album, [The Hurting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hurting). The [Gary Jules & Michael Andrews](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gary_Jules) version is from their 2002 album, [Donnie Darko (soundtrack)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donnie_Darko_(soundtrack)).

Michael Andrews stripped the song down to piano, mellotron and a touch of vocoder for the Donnie Darko soundtrack. Gary Jules delivered the fragile vocal that made it stick. The pairing turned a moody synth-pop deep cut into a slow devastating ballad and gave the UK its Christmas number one in 2003, twenty years after the original charted.
